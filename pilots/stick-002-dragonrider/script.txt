You open your eyes for the very first time on Dragonstone. You're a Targaryen. Congratulations. Before you can even crawl, your mother puts a dragon egg in your cradle. If you're lucky, it hatches. Yours doesn't. You spend your childhood cuddling a very expensive rock.

At twelve, you do what dragonless Targaryens do. You walk into the Dragonmont, alone, to claim a wild dragon. Nobody calls it a test, but it is one. Plenty of your cousins walked in. Fewer walked out.

The dragon smells you first. You raise your hand and say one word in High Valyrian. It lowers its head. You're a dragonrider now. No saddle. No training. Just a two-ton fire lizard that does not take directions.

For a few years, it's the best life in the world. Then your family starts a civil war over a chair.

Dragons fight dragons. Riders burn. And over Rook's Rest, you learn the one rule nobody taught you. Dragons outlive their riders.
