Picture this. It's payday, and by Thursday your account is already in the red. Sound familiar?

Here's what nobody tells you. The first thousand dollars you save changes more than almost any raise.

Without it, every surprise is an emergency. A flat tire goes on a credit card. A doctor's bill turns into a payday loan, and that loan quietly costs you another hundred, two hundred dollars in fees.

With a thousand dollars set aside, that same flat tire is just an annoying Tuesday. You pay it, and you move on. No interest. No panic.

And something changes in your head, too. You stop checking your balance before every purchase. You sleep better. You start making choices instead of reacting.

So start small. Twenty-five dollars a week gets you there in under ten months.
