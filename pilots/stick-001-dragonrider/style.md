# Style rules — stick channel ("Why It Sucks To Be…")

- **Our own stick-figure look** (genre-standard round heads, not a copy of any channel's designs):
  round white head, two tiny black dot eyes, a small simple line mouth, thin black line arms and legs,
  a small rounded torso in clothing, thick clean black outlines, flat colors, simple flat backgrounds.
- **Recurring hero (this video):** young Targaryen, **silver-white hair**, dark red-and-black tunic.
- Dragons and big creatures are drawn **more detailed than the stick figures** (scale = comedy + threat).
- **Simple moves only** (done in the edit): slide in/out, walk across, bob, tremble, fly across, fall. **No zoom.**
- Moving characters are generated on a **solid flat pure green background** and keyed out.
- No text, letters or numbers in pictures. Eyes: both the same state; closed eyes via eye-only edit.
- Narrator: Kokoro `am_onyx`, speed 0.93 (calm, dry). Different from the finance channel on purpose.
