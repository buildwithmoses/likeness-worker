You open your eyes for the very first time on Dragonstone, a Targaryen. Congratulations. Before you can even crawl, your mother places a dragon egg in your cradle. It's tradition. If you're lucky, it hatches. If you're not, you just spent your childhood cuddling a very expensive rock.

Yours never hatches. So at twelve, you do the only other thing a dragonless Targaryen can do. You walk into the Dragonmont, alone, to claim a wild dragon. Nobody calls this a test, but it is one. Plenty of your cousins walked in. Some of them walked out.

The dragon smells you before it sees you. You raise your hand and whisper one word in High Valyrian. It lowers its head. Congratulations, you're a dragonrider. You also have no saddle, no training, and a two-ton fire lizard that does not take directions.

For a few years, it's the best life in the world. Then your family starts a civil war over a chair.

Dragons fight dragons. Riders burn. And on the day the sky over Rook's Rest catches fire, you learn the one rule nobody taught you. Dragons outlive their riders.
